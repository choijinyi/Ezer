ABSTRACT
The purpose of this writing is to study how soccer mission activity as
Sports culture influences the church and evaluate positive factors of soccer
mission activity. The Research Questions of this study: ①As a Sports culture,
does soccer mission activity have a function of strengthening the intimacy of
community? ②As a Sports culture, how does soccer mission activity influence
evangelism, service and volunteer work? ③As a Sports culture, does soccer
mission activity have connecting function between effective evangelism and
mission? ④As a Sports culture, does soccer mission activity have strengthening
function of mission strategy for the next generation? ⑤As a Sports culture,
does soccer mission activity influence relationship with senior pastor? ⑥As a
Sports culture, does soccer mission activity help spiritual maturity? ⑦As a
Sports culture, does soccer mission provide psychological stability? These
causes will be considered in order to contribute to church restoration
movement. A variety of problems will be minimized and apply the study to church
by finding meaningful and beneficial causes.

believers to leave church and a dearth of appropriate policies for children
